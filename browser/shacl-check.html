Public SHACL checker — UK Parliament SPARQL endpoint

Validate live data from the public Parliament triple store (api.parliament.uk/sparql) against SHACL shapes, entirely in your browser. Built on Google's schemarama (vendored under third_party/schemarama).

How do you SHACL-check a database rather than a web page? You don't validate the whole store. A SPARQL CONSTRUCT/DESCRIBE query carves out the subgraph you care about — one resource, or a sample of a class — and returns it as an RDF document. That document is what SHACL validates. The extraction query below is the scoping knob: change it to check a different "bit" of the database.
