Secondary legislation, mapped

Two layers here are defined by secondary legislation, over an OpenStreetMap / open-elevation base — focused on the Calder Valley constituency.

Constituency boundary — set by the Parliamentary Constituencies Order 2023 (an SI).
Flood Warning Area — statutory EA warning footprint.
Flood Alert Area — broader river catchment.
◍ Settlement (Wikidata) — population & elevation.
Why is this “secondary legislation”?

Westminster constituency boundaries are made by an Order in Council — a statutory instrument — laid under the Parliamentary Constituencies Act 1986. Flood warning/alert areas are the EA's statutory designations under the Flood and Water Management Act 2010 & the Civil Contingencies Act 2004. legislation.gov.uk records the text and jurisdictional extent (E/W/S/NI); the polygons live in the geo datasets joined here by name / GSS code.
