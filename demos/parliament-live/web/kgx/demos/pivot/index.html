Pivot Viewer — UK Prime Ministers

Inspired by Microsoft's 2010 Silverlight Pivot Viewer: pick a facet to pivot by and the tiles rearrange into labeled groups — the spiritual move from grid view to histogram view. Plus Freebase-Parallax-style "navigate to" buttons that build the next query from the visible set. ~300 lines of vanilla JS; no SDK.
