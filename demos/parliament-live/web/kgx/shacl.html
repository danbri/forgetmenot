SHACL checker

Validate live data from a SPARQL endpoint against SHACL shapes, in the browser. Built on Google's schemarama. Defaults to this server's bundled store; switch to UK Parliament's DDP endpoint or any other from the registry.

You don't validate a whole database — you validate a subgraph. A SPARQL CONSTRUCT/DESCRIBE carves out the slice you care about (one resource, or a sample of a class) and returns it as RDF; that is what SHACL checks. The extraction query below is the scoping knob. Sampling with LIMIT is a spot-check, not a full-store audit.
