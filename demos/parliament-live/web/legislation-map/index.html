The UK's secondary-legislation geographies

Every layer here is a geography whose boundary is drawn by secondary legislation — a statutory instrument made under an Act of Parliament. These designations are mostly devolved, so the governing instrument differs by nation (England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland). Toggle the layers in the control (top-right); each feature's popup names the instrument that applies in its nation and links legislation.gov.uk.

Westminster constituencies (UK) — Parliamentary Constituencies Order 2023 (SI 2023/1230); reserved, so one UK-wide instrument covers all 650 (incl. NI's 18).
Marine protected areas (UK) — England/Wales/NI MCZs + Scotland Nature Conservation MPAs. Different Act per nation (see popups).
National Parks (GB) — England/Wales (NPACA 1949) + Scotland (Nat. Parks (Scotland) Act 2000). Northern Ireland has no national parks (none have ever been designated).
AONBs / National Scenic Areas (UK) — England/Wales AONBs (CROW 2000), NI AONBs (NCALO 1985), Scotland's analogue NSAs. Differing instrument per nation.
COVID-19 restrictions, 2 Dec 2020 — devolved & divergent:
England tiers (SI 2020/1374): 1 2 3
Scotland levels (SSI 2020/344): 1 2 3 4
Wales & NI — own regimes, shown national-level (popups link them).
Why is this “secondary legislation drawing a map”?

A statutory instrument can do more than write rules — it can draw a boundary. Constituency boundaries are set by an Order in Council; each marine zone, National Park, National Landscape / AONB and National Scenic Area is created by its own designation order under a nation-specific Act. The COVID case is the starkest — and the most divergent: England's tier schedule listed which councils sat in which tier, but Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland each ran an entirely separate legal regime. The legislation is the map, but there were four maps.

Sources & honesty notes

Constituencies, council/country boundaries, England COVID LADs: Office for National Statistics, Open Geography Portal (ONS / OS, OGL v3.0 + © Crown copyright). England nature layers: Natural England / JNCC (OGL v3.0). Scotland: NatureScot (National Parks, Nature Conservation MPAs); National Scenic Areas via an ArcGIS republish of NatureScot data. Wales: Natural Resources Wales / DataMapWales (OGL v3.0). Northern Ireland: DAERA / OSNI “SpatialNI” (AONBs, MCZs; OGL v3.0). Legislative instruments: legislation.gov.uk (© Crown copyright, OGL v3.0). Basemaps: © OpenStreetMap contributors; OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA).

Honesty: Northern Ireland has no national parks, so it is genuinely absent from that one layer (stated above, not silently dropped). Scotland's COVID protection levels are transcribed from the gov.scot allocation review in force on 2 Dec 2020; Wales and NI are shown as a single national fill because no per-area choropleth was sourced for that date — their popups link the relevant nation's own regulations rather than inventing per-area data. Boundaries are server-side generalised for web display — not authoritative for legal extent. No UK Parliament API data is used on this page.

Every layer traces upstream. “oh yeah?” reveals the exact source query, the skills/ wrapper used (where one exists), and the governing instrument — so any datum here can be followed back. Full method & per-nation counts: README · all wrappers: skills/.
