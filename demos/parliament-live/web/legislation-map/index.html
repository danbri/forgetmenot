England's secondary-legislation geographies

Every layer here is a geography whose boundary is drawn by secondary legislation — a statutory instrument (SI) made under an Act of Parliament. Toggle them in the layer control (top-right). Each feature's popup links the governing instrument on legislation.gov.uk.

Marine Conservation Zones — each a named MCZ Designation Order SI.
National Parks — designation orders (NPACA 1949).
National Landscapes / AONBs — designation orders (CROW Act 2000).

Why is this “secondary legislation drawing a map”?

A statutory instrument can do more than write rules — it can draw a boundary. Constituency boundaries are set by an Order in Council; each Marine Conservation Zone, National Park and National Landscape is created by its own designation order. The COVID tier schedule is the starkest case: Schedule 4 of SI 2020/1374 literally lists which council areas sit in Tier 2 and Tier 3 — the legislation is the map. Anything not listed defaulted to Tier 1 (reg. 8).
